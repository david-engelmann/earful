# Slice 16. A postscript on the design record

DESIGN.md is the Phase 1 record and reads in the future
tense in its closing sections, because no repository
existed when it was written. Read cold, it misleads about
what has since happened.

## What landed

- A dated postscript at the foot of DESIGN.md, leaving
  the record itself untouched. It states the repository's
  creation and rename, the one change of build order Main
  ruled, the report format as built at earful-themes/0.2
  with the three fields the record does not name, the
  closing of the sentence splitter defect the record
  carries, and the rerun proof standing where the record
  proposed a golden test.
