# Slice 28. A mechanical audit of the stack

Twenty four branches were about to be handed over for
landing on the strength of their individual records.
Nothing had checked the stack as a stack.

## What landed

- docs/STACK-AUDIT.md records three mechanical checks
  run against the hosted state. The full tree at the
  top holds all 92 expected files. The first parent
  chain walked from the top contains every branch tip
  except two, both explained. Every head carries a
  hosted green run on its exact head, the proof branch
  excepted as recorded.
- The two findings are stated for the landing. The
  proof branch's whole contribution already stands in
  the top's tree with identical blob hashes, and it
  should not be landed as its own pull request. The
  labelling cousin joint is confirmed contained, its
  five differing files all carried by the top in later
  forms.
- The documents index names the audit in its evidence
  section and gains its slice 28 line, and the
  changelog records it.
